### Procrastination

How can I beat procrastination? Many of us struggle with it everyday dealing with the guilt of being lazy.
We feel that laziness  eats up our soul bit by bit and we don't do anything about it. Over time I have figured out my own way of beating procrastination. It's to not be alone or at least get outside.
I think it works most times, but it is not cost effective because you are outsourcing someone to be with you. This is not a fight won by yourself with your will power. Naturally our brain does not like to do hard things.
