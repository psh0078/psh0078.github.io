### What is this writing about?

This is my testimony and an on-going fight of mine that I deal with everyday.
Procrastination is my biggest enemy as it is for most of us. Most people never practice discipline.
They are slaves to instant gratification and laziness. Most of us will die with
a unfulfilled purpose, not living to the fullness of their life.

### Procrastination

How can I beat procrastination? Many of us struggle with it everyday dealing
with the guilt of being lazy. We feel that laziness eats up our soul bit by bit
and we don't do anything about it. Over time I have figured out my own way of
beating procrastination. It's to not be alone or at least get outside. I think
it works most times, but it is not cost effective because you are outsourcing
someone to be with you. This is not a fight won by yourself with your will
power. Naturally our brain does not like to do hard things.

### Waking up in the morning

To me, conquering the day means conquering the morning.
