I hereby put an end to my delicate, soft loser's mindset with which I risked
nothing to become greater. I was scared. I have to adopt a new mindset which I
called hustler's mind. It's about going through the pain and sacrificing a lot
of what I can enjoy right now for my growth at an exponential rate.

How many hours are people actually productive at work? I guess the range could
vary for people, the average person is productive for about 3 hours of an
8-hour workday. Hustlers try their best to maximize their productivity during
that 8-hour period, and it is really hard. I can name a couple reasons. Not
many people have an owner's mindset, meaning they are merely there for their
fixed salary. For them, minimizing the amount of work is the most ideal thing
they could do. Unlike our parents generation, this enough-for-today mindset is
ingrained in our young generation. Secondly, these days people have a short
attention span because they are slaves to short form content.

However, hustlers think differently. They try to make their work meaningful.
They care about their growth and impact. Simply putting, the only thing they
care about is their output.

Minimizing the amount of energy spent on trivial things is their habit.
Thinking about what to eat for lunch and dinner is not worth a second of their
time.
