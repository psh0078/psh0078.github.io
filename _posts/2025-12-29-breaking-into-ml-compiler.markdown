In my free time, I want to study and research ML compilers with the hope of it
coming to intersect with my current research topic in the future.

I have always felt the itch of getting to the bottom of computer science:
low-level systems. With the AI hype, my attention has naturally been drawn to
machine learning compilers, where hardware meets software. This is the field
where lots of things are happening in the industry right now. The hottest topic
is the CUDA platform by NVIDIA dominating the AI world. Due to its monopoly,
most AI developers and engineers have to deal with NVIDIA GPUs or CUDA.
But there is a limitation to what one can do with their hardware because of all
these things that are black-boxed by NVIDIA at low level. For example, parallel
thread execution (PTX), which is a virtual machine ISA for CUDA, has only been
partly documented. It leaves developers helpless. The usual goal
for AI model developers is to optimize every operations on GPU as much as possible.

However, facing limitations in the work by one monopolizing company with their
proprietary software seems just ridiculus and not a viable solution in the
grand scheme of things. Then, what can we do?
